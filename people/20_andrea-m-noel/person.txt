Bio: <p><strong>Andrea M. Noel</strong>, PhD, is Professor Emerita in the Department of Childhood and Early Childhood Education at the State University of New York at New Paltz. She has expertise in Child Learning and Development and has much international experience, including Australia, Germany, as a Fulbright Scholar in Hungary, and most recently as a grant recipient from the Polish National Agency for Academic Exchange. Dr. Noel was chosen as a Sustainability Fellow at SUNY New Paltz and infused sustainability content into almost all her classes. She has published widely in the fields of early childhood education and learning.</p>
