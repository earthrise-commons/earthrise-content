Bio: <h2>Creative Exploration Facilitator at Stepping Stones Museum for Children, Visitor Center Associate at The Glass House, National Trust for Historical Preservation</h2><p>Isabella&nbsp;Grecco&nbsp;is a museum educator and social justice researcher based in Norwalk, Connecticut. She earned her Master’s degree in Social Justice Education from SUNY New Paltz, where her work focused on equity, critical pedagogy, and community-based learning. She is an enthusiastic post-qualitative researcher, all through self-published zines! She is dedicated to exploring how culture, education, and storytelling can serve as tools for sustainability and anti-racist practice, among other social justice-related goals!&nbsp;</p>
