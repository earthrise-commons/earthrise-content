  headline: About Earthrise Commons
  content: '<p>Earthrise Commons will serve as a digital resource companion to our Open Access book, featuring projects that highlight how sustainability education and community engagement are being integrated into courses, programs, and community initiatives worldwide. These submissions will celebrate diverse approaches to sustainability education and offer actionable insights and models that can be adapted by other institutions and communities to enhance learner engagement, equity, and impact.</p><p>Selected submissions are a form of publishing that you can use to build community, advance ideas, and sprinkle some sustainability learning and community engagement into your teaching, no matter your field of study.</p>'

  headline: Why Contribute?
  content: '<h3><strong>By sharing your work, you will:</strong></h3><ul><li><p>Join a local and global community of sustainability educators and practitioners</p></li><li><p>Contribute to open educational resources for sustainability action</p></li><li><p>Connect with peers through our collaborative webinar series</p></li><li><p>Help build a more sustainable and equitable future</p></li></ul><p>We’re transforming a forthcoming anthology, Sustainability Learning for Action &amp; Community Engagement (Palgrave Macmillan SpringerNature), into a living sustainability education platform called the Earthrise Commons. This Open Education Resource will provide educators worldwide (high school through college undergraduate) with free, accessible, adaptable, and shareable content to support sustainability education and action.</p><h3><strong>The platform will include:</strong></h3><ul><li><p>A Creative Commons License:</p><ul><li><p>CC-BY-NC</p></li><li><p>CC-BY-SA</p></li><li><p>CC-BY</p></li></ul></li><li><p>Original book chapters via SpringerLink (with metrics)</p></li><li><p>Lesson plans that educators can modify</p></li><li><p>Interactive resources and videos</p></li><li><p>A toolbox of sustainability competencies and frameworks</p></li><li><p>Student work examples (with permission)</p></li></ul><p>The project uses an open-source infrastructure, managed by a team of curators at SUNY New Paltz, U.S. The design prioritizes search ability, follows UN Global Goals taxonomies, and supports Universal Design for Learning principles. This approach activates sustainability education in a collaborative, interdisciplinary learning space where knowledge can be shared and built upon by the community.</p><h3><strong>Each submission may also include:</strong></h3><ul><li><p>A personal bio page (optional, this can be as short as two lines)</p></li></ul><p>On your bio page, you may provide:</p><ul><li><p>Links to more resources</p></li><li><p>An indication that you’re open to being contacted about the project</p></li><li><p>An email contact method</p></li></ul>'

  headline: Submission Guidelines
  content: '<p>We welcome submissions that address innovative and impactful sustainability initiatives across social, environmental, and economic dimensions.</p><p>Submissions should include:</p><ul><li><p><strong>Overview:</strong> A general description of the initiative, including its purpose and context within sustainability’s multiple dimensions</p></li><li><p><strong>Case Study:</strong> A detailed example illustrating how the initiative was implemented and its outcomes</p></li><li><p><strong>Equity and Access:</strong> How the initiative addresses&nbsp;human flourishing&nbsp;and access for diverse populations</p></li><li><p><strong>Impacts:</strong> Share any assessment or desired outcomes of this approach</p></li><li><p><strong>Adaptability:</strong> Guidance for how the approach can be tailored to other contexts, communities, or learning environments</p></li><li><p><strong>Additional Resources:</strong> Any tools, frameworks, or materials that support design and implementation</p></li></ul><p>Your submission should consider:</p><ul><li><p><strong>Community Access:</strong> How your project enables equitable participation</p></li><li><p><strong>Potential Barriers:</strong> Solutions for overcoming obstacles to participation</p></li><li><p><strong>Allocating Resources:</strong> Costs and funding strategies</p></li><li><p><strong>Knowledge Sharing:</strong> Training and preparing participants</p></li></ul>'
