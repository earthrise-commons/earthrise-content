Summary: <p>In a world facing environmental and socio-economic challenges, Build Green Now (BGN) offers a strong message of hope and action. This is a clarion call for global collaboration and sustainable development, emphasizing the transformative potential of industrial hemp. Quoting Jack Herer, “Hemp will be the future of all mankind, or there won’t be a future,” BGN highlights the urgency of adopting hemp for green building and entrepreneurship. Educators are key to shifting the narrative from despair to hope by inspiring future generations to embrace sustainability. BGN's initiatives empower communities with knowledge and tools for sustainable urban development, leveraging hemp's multifaceted benefits. Henry Gage, Jr. and Sally Warren provide a strategic roadmap for collective action, detailing considerations and case studies that highlight hemp's role in creating resilient communities. From its soil-rejuvenating properties to its potential in producing eco-friendly products, hemp is a versatile and essential resource for a sustainable future. Combining legislative advocacy, community engagement with public benefit contracts, and educational initiatives, BGN envisions a world where economic stability and environmental health go hand in hand. In 2026, Build Green Now launches Voices for Progress at voicesforprogress.net—a global platform powered by MaayaData connecting communities worldwide to create a future worth believing in.</p>
